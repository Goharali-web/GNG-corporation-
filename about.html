OUR MISSION

Designed to Architect.

GNG Corporation exists at the intersection of mathematical automation and premium web aesthetics.

PHILOSOPHY

Frictionless pipelines, gorgeous outputs.

We believe traditional business software is slow, cluttered, and disconnected. Staff spend hours manual-entering text across isolated platforms, and user-facing web layers feel static and outdated.

GNG bridges this gap. We combine secure API automation (AI Agents) with rich immersive interfaces (3D WebGL designs) to engineer experiences that work in the background and engage customers instantly.

Our Core Principles

  • 01. Minimal Design — generous margins, simple grids, high contrast.
  • 02. Code Sovereignty — we write clean, vanilla scripts, avoiding bloated templates.
  • 03. System Security — strict token compliance protocols.
THE WORKFLOW

A Systematic Integration Process

How we take your projects from initial architectural drafts to final launch telemetry.

01

Scoping Audit

We conduct a 30-minute technical evaluation mapping your data pipelines, email structures, and customer chat touchpoints to spot manual bottlenecks.

02

Design Prototyping

Our UI designers deliver high-fidelity interactive wireframes. For 3D tracks, we map initial Spline canvas prototypes before writing code.

03

System Integration

We construct and test your system: establishing OAuth tokens, compiling SQL databases, configuring custom prompts, and scripting client structures.

04

Telemetry Launch

After thorough verification, we deploy files to your host. We link live error monitoring logs and telemetry tracking panels to secure your workspace.

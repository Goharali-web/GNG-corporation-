THE SOLUTION

Automated Email Triaging

An autonomous mail workflow engine that processes, categorizes, drafts, and archives incoming mail.

Intent Classification

Scans incoming payloads and sorts them into High Priority, FAQ support, Partnership, or Spam using prompt-engineered classifications.

Draft Preparation

Generates polished responses in your brand's unique tone and saves them directly in your "Drafts" folder for quick human-approval sending.

Persistent Follow-Ups

Tracks unanswered inquiries and triggers gentle, context-aware follow-ups after 3, 5, or 10 days to keep your pipeline warm.

In modern mobile commerce, a delayed response is a lost sale. Customers expect instant answers on messaging platforms. If you force prospective clients to wait hours for a catalog item confirmation or scheduling availability, they go to your competitor.

THE SOLUTION

Programmatic Conversational Pipelines

We integrate customized LLM architectures directly with the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API.

Semantic Syncing

The agent maps customer requests to your live inventory schemas, CRM databases, or PDFs, ensuring answers are grounded in raw facts.

Structured Actions

Not just chat: the agent triggers external webhooks, updates CRM pipelines, schedules calendar events, and parses user data.

Human-in-the-Loop

Seamlessly hands off high-value conversations to live customer support representatives when human discretion is required.
